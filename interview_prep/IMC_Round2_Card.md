# IMC round 2 — one page

## Opener, 4 sentences. Learn these, improvise the rest.
1. "Senior engineer at Capital.com, three and a half years. I lead two engineers."
2. "I own the client communication platform behind our CRM — email, push, in-app inbox. **Millions of messages a day.**"
3. "Go workers pump the queue; the **Python executor** and the **governance service** in front of it are mine. I write Python. I design and review Go and Java, I don't write them."
4. "Best piece: re-architecting the send path. Million-scale campaigns from 5-6 hours to about one, push throughput 8-10x, and it killed a bug where we reported failed deliveries as succeeded."

## Say first, before they ask
"My README says 'fewest bank transfers'. That's an overclaim — greedy gives at most n−1, not the minimum. `A=-4, B=-3, C=2, D=2, E=3` → my code gives 4, the optimum is 3."

## Numbers
2,325,046 records → 5,702 transfers · 8s · 29 MB · 17 tests · 121 lines · stdlib only

## Three don'ts
- Don't open with the Debezium pipeline — it's Java and not your code.
- Don't inflate Go or Java. Ever.
- Don't say "the tool suggested it".

## "What do you dislike?"
"Work that's structurally reactive. Not being on call for what I ship — I think you should be. I mean where firefighting is the job instead of a signal to go fix the cause."

## Best question to ask them
"What did you actually look at in my submission, and what would have made you more comfortable?"
